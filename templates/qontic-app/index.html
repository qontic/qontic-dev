Template behavior

The shared controls define consistent behavior and appearance. Apps connect their physics engine through callbacks or the standard qontic events.

Control contract

The interpretation selector occupies the full control-panel width. The next row contains Start/Stop, the two-state automatic-rerun control, and Speed. App parameters appear in attached Core, Advanced, and Display tabs. Results use an optional draggable, collapsible panel over the canvas.
