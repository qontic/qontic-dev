Template behavior

The shared controls define consistent behavior and appearance. Apps connect their physics engine through callbacks or the standard qontic events.

Control contract

The combined run control toggles Play/Pause and contains Restart and New Run. Representation names are fixed as Orthodox, Pilot Wave, and Many Worlds. Additional controls use Core, Advanced, and Display. Results appear in an optional draggable, collapsible panel over the canvas.
