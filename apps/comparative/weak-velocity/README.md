Weak Velocity in the Double Slit

Part of the Q-Ontic Lab project. Wiseman's weak-velocity protocol behind the
analytical double slit: a weak position measurement (separate pointer), a
strong position measurement a short gap later, and post-selection on the
strong result. The wave and its coefficients come from
../double-slit-analytical/js/source-packet-model.js.

Tests: node apps/comparative/weak-velocity/tests/weak-model.test.mjs
