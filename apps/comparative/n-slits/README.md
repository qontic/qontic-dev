N-Slits (4.0)

This simulation is part of the Q-Ontic Lab project.

The double slit and beyond: 1 to 8 slits, which-slit detectors and slits closed
during a run, with three views of one model (Orthodox, Pilot Wave, Many Worlds),
three levels (Basic, Standard, Expert) and two engines for the same geometry:
the analytical paraxial model and the Schrödinger equation on a grid (WebGL2).

Live version:
https://qonticlab.rice.edu/apps/comparative/n-slits/

Development version:
https://qontic.github.io/qontic-dev/apps/comparative/n-slits/

Replaces N-Slits 3.0 (archive/n-slit-analytical-3.0) and the numerical
double-slit and N-slit apps (archive/double-slit-webgl-app,
archive/n-slit-numerical-preview); their old addresses redirect here.
