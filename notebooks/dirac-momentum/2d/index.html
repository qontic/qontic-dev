DiracPacketSplitting2D

Theory

This is a 2D free-Dirac analogue of the Gaussian splitting scheme discussed in the accompanying 1D paper. A localized packet with mean wave vector k0 is multiplied by one fixed spinor. Unless that spinor lies entirely in one energy band, the packet already contains both positive- and negative-energy components.

iℏ∂tψ = [c(σxpx + σypy) + mc2σz]ψ

For a narrow packet around k0, the two branches have approximately the same mean momentum but opposite energies and opposite group velocities:

E± = ±√[(mc2)2 + (ℏc|k0|)2],    v± ≈ ± ℏc2k0E0

±E mix angle controls the fixed spinor relative to the central positive-energy eigenspinor. At 0° the central mode is +E, at 180° it is -E, and at 90° the central momentum is split 50/50 between the two branches. Because the spinor is fixed while the Gaussian has finite momentum width, the exact spectral populations shown in the stats can differ slightly from the central weights.

The yellow dots are Bohmian positions guided by the full 2D Dirac current:

ρ = ψ†ψ,    j = cψ†(σx,σy)ψ,    ṙ = jρ

As the two wave packets separate, trajectories become dominated by one local branch and approach motion roughly parallel or antiparallel to k0. The particle-direction counts in the stats are instantaneous, not a branch measurement.

Both the FFT wave evolution and the Bohmian particles use periodic boundary conditions. The cyan rectangle marks one fundamental cell of the torus. When a packet or particle exits one side, it re-enters from the opposite side.
