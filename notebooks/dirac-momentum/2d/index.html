Asymptotic momentum

Theory

1D paper mode reproduces the paper's setup inside the 2D renderer: the state is uniform in y, its mean momentum is along x, and Bohmian motion is longitudinal. Turning it off restores a localized, genuinely 2D extension. Displayed values use ℏ=1.

iℏ∂tψ = [c(σxpx + σypy) + mc2σz]ψ

The initial angles Θ0 and Ω0 set the spinor in the paper's convention. The recommended preset starts with Θ0=90°, Ω0=0° and then lets the positive- and negative-energy parts separate.

For a narrow packet around p0=ℏk0, both branches retain approximately the same momentum while their energies and velocities have opposite signs:

E± = ±√[(mc2)2 + (ℏc|k0|)2],    v± ≈ ± ℏc2k0E0

The dots are Bohmian positions guided by the Dirac current. Full 2D mode uses both current components; 1D paper mode uses only the longitudinal projection and holds y fixed. The three colored dots are representative initial-position quantiles and are followed in the trajectory plots.

ρ = ψ†ψ,    j = cψ†(σx,σy)ψ,    ṙ = jρ

The displayed Bohm/Vigier energy and momentum are the natural bilinear form of the paper's 1D quantities in this representation:

S=ψ†σzψ,   EB=mc2ρS,   pB=mjS

The diagnostic is singular at S=0. For a readable educational plot, the displayed Bohmian distributions are explicitly conditioned on |S|/ρ≥0.06; finite values closer to the singularity are excluded rather than clipped. The dashboard reports both the mask-kept and charted counts. The momentum histogram overlays the conserved canonical FFT distribution, while velocity and energy develop peaks near ±v0 and ±E0.

Simulation scale enlarges both the physical domain and its longitudinal grid while preserving spatial resolution. The 4× full-2D setting is intentionally demanding because the FFT solver runs on the CPU.

The FFT domain is periodic. Paper-style conclusions apply only before the clean-time warning; after a packet wraps around, the two branches meet and interfere again.
