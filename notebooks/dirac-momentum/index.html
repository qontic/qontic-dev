Dirac Momentum

Free Dirac wave packets can split into positive- and negative-energy branches with the same central momentum but opposite group velocities.

Theory & Equations

Free Dirac evolution

The packet evolves without a scalar potential. In three dimensions the free Dirac equation is:

Free Dirac equation \[ i\hbar\frac{\partial\psi}{\partial t} = \left[-i\hbar c\,\boldsymbol{\alpha}\cdot\nabla + mc^2\beta\right]\psi . \]

Branch velocities

The two free branches have opposite dispersion slopes. For the same momentum, the sign of the energy branch reverses the group velocity.

Energy and group velocity \[ E_{\pm}(\mathbf{p}) = \pm\sqrt{m^2c^4+c^2|\mathbf{p}|^2}, \qquad \mathbf{v}_{g,\pm} = \pm \frac{c^2\mathbf{p}}{\sqrt{m^2c^4+c^2|\mathbf{p}|^2}} . \]

Bohmian guidance

The particles in both applets are guided by the Dirac current of the full spinor field.

Dirac density and current \[ \rho=\psi^\dagger\psi,\qquad \mathbf{j}=c\,\psi^\dagger\boldsymbol{\alpha}\psi,\qquad \dot{\mathbf{X}}=\frac{\mathbf{j}}{\rho}. \]
