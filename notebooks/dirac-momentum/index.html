Dirac momentum

Free Dirac wave packets can split into positive- and negative-energy branches with the same central momentum but opposite group velocities.

You will need: the relativistic energy E² = m²c⁴ + c²p², and the idea of pilot-wave theory that particles move along the current of their wave. The 3D simulation needs a browser with WebGPU.

Theory and equations

Free Dirac evolution, branch velocities, guidance, and the momentum diagnostic.

Free Dirac evolution

The packet evolves without a scalar potential. In three dimensions the free Dirac equation is:

Free Dirac equation \[ i\hbar\frac{\partial\psi}{\partial t} = \left[-i\hbar c\,\boldsymbol{\alpha}\cdot\nabla + mc^2\beta\right]\psi . \]

Branch velocities

The two free branches have opposite dispersion slopes. For the same momentum, the sign of the energy branch reverses the group velocity.

Energy and group velocity \[ E_{\pm}(\mathbf{p}) = \pm\sqrt{m^2c^4+c^2|\mathbf{p}|^2}, \qquad \mathbf{v}_{g,\pm} = \pm \frac{c^2\mathbf{p}}{\sqrt{m^2c^4+c^2|\mathbf{p}|^2}} . \]

Bohmian guidance

The particles are guided by the Dirac current of the full spinor field. The 2D paper preset takes its longitudinal projection and keeps the replicated transverse coordinate fixed.

Dirac density and current \[ \rho=\psi^\dagger\psi,\qquad \mathbf{j}=c\,\psi^\dagger\boldsymbol{\alpha}\psi,\qquad \dot{\mathbf{X}}=\frac{\mathbf{j}}{\rho}. \]

Trajectory momentum and energy

In the two-component 2D representation, the paper's Bohm/Vigier quantities have the following bilinear form. They return the same momentum on both separated energy branches, even though the branch velocities have opposite signs.

Bohm/Vigier diagnostic used in 2D \[ S=\psi^\dagger\sigma_z\psi, \qquad E_B=mc^2\frac{\rho}{S}, \qquad \mathbf p_B=m\frac{\mathbf j}{S}, \qquad \mathbf v=\frac{c^2\mathbf p_B}{E_B}. \]

The educational histograms are explicitly conditioned on \(|S|/\rho\ge0.06\) to stay away from the singularity, and stop being interpreted as asymptotic once periodic wrap-around begins.
